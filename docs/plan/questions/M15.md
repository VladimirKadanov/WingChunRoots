M15 did not stop on these questions: each keeps its proposed default, marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT …` and docs/adr/0018-organizations.md), until the owner answers it.

## 2026-11-02 — M15 — What may an organization edit before and after approval

**Question:** The brief says a pending organization can only edit its own application. What may an approved organization change without a new review?

**Options:**
1. **Pending: every field of its application, and nothing else (no admins, members or schools). Approved: the website, the leader and the number of schools; the official name, the kind and the country stay as the reviewers approved them (a change goes through the administrators).**
2. Approved: every field, and a change of name, kind or country reopens the review (as a school listing's edit does in M11).
3. Approved: nothing; every change goes through the administrators.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`REVIEWED_FIELDS` in `src/server/services/organizations.ts`; ADR 0018 §4). Option 2 would add a reopened `organization_application` case on such an edit.

## 2026-11-02 — M15 — Who decides an organization's application

**Question:** Which reviewers approve or decline an organization's application?

**Options:**
1. **An archivist or an administrator, as an instructor verification (identity and authenticity); any reviewer may take the case, ask the applicant and add notes.**
2. Administrators only.
3. Any reviewer, moderators included (as a school listing).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`DECIDERS.organization_application` in `src/server/services/review-core.ts`).

## 2026-11-02 — M15 — Who answers a school link, and the platform's own schools

**Question:** An organization asks a school to link to it. Who answers for the school, and what about the schools the platform keeps without an account (the demo schools of M01)?

**Options:**
1. **The account that listed the school (`school.created_by_user_id`) accepts or refuses; a school without such an account cannot be asked. Either side ends an accepted link; the organization withdraws a pending one. The school and its pin never change.**
2. Also the school's verified instructors may answer.
3. An administrator answers for the platform's schools.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`requestOrganizationSchool` and `answerSchoolLink` in `src/server/services/organizations.ts`).
