# M20 — questions for the owner

M20 did not stop on these questions: each keeps its proposed default, marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT D17`, `DEFAULT D18` and docs/adr/0023-school-activity.md), until the owner answers it.

## 2026-11-08 — M20 — What counts as activity

**Question:** Which actions of the listing account reset the 90/105-day clock of a listed school?

**Options:**
1. **An edit that really changes the listing (`schools.update`), sending it for review (`schools.submit`) and "This listing is current" (`schools.confirm`). Signing in, opening the dashboard, adding photos or answering an organization link do not count.**
2. The same, plus any change of the school's photos or organization links.
3. Any sign-in of the listing account.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (DEFAULT D17; `ACTIVITY_RESET` in `src/server/services/school-activity.ts`, spread by `updateSchool` only on a real change, `submitSchool` and `confirmSchool`). An edit that saves the same details is not activity. The integration test checks that a sign-in moves nothing.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-08 — M20 — The thresholds and a missed run

**Question:** When is a listing asked to be confirmed, and when is it hidden, if the daily job did not run for some days?

**Options:**
1. **Day 90 without activity: awaiting confirmation (the owner is asked; still public). Day 105: hidden, but never less than 14 days after the request, so a job that missed days asks first and hides two weeks later. Ages are measured with the database clock.**
2. Day 90 and day 105 strictly, even if the request went out on day 104.
3. Other thresholds (for example 180/195).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`SCHOOL_AWAITING_DAYS = 90`, `SCHOOL_HIDDEN_DAYS = 105`, `SCHOOL_CONFIRM_GRACE_DAYS = 14` in `src/server/db/schema/common.ts`; `hiddenFrom()` gives the date the notice shows).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-08 — M20 — What a confirmation brings back

**Question:** When a hidden listing is confirmed or edited, does the pin always come back?

**Options:**
1. **The stage always returns to `current`, but the pin comes back only if every other condition still holds: the listing is active and approved, and the listing account holds Professional Sifu. A Free Sifu's confirmation is recorded and unlocks no pin. A listing that moderation returned to draft cannot be confirmed (it goes through review again).**
2. The pin comes back for every plan, as a reward for confirming.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (DEFAULT D18; `v_public_school` decides; the owner's page says "Your confirmation is recorded either way. A public pin also needs Professional Sifu.").

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-08 — M20 — Schools without a listing account

**Question:** The fictional schools of M01 are records of the platform with no listing account (`created_by_user_id` is null). Should the check hide them?

**Options:**
1. **No: nobody can be asked or can confirm, so the check leaves them alone. The staff page shows them as "a record of the platform, not checked".**
2. Hide them after 105 days like any other listing.

## 2026-11-08 — M20 — Who is told, and how

**Question:** Who gets the notices of the activity check, and may they turn the e-mail off?

**Options:**
1. **The owner gets "Is your school listing still current?" on day 90 and "Your school listing is not shown for now" on day 105. The admins of the active organizations with an accepted link to the school get the day-90 notice for information only (they cannot confirm). Both are in the `schools` category: in the dashboard always, by e-mail by default, and the e-mail can be turned off (M18).**
2. The owner only.
3. The same, and the e-mail cannot be turned off.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`school.activity-awaiting`, `school.activity-hidden` in `src/server/templates/notifications.ts`; `mailed('schools', 'notice')` in the catalog). The copy gives the last activity and an approximate date, with no countdown or urgency (CLAUDE.md §11).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)
