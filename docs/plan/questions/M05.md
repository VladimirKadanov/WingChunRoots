# M05 — questions for the owner

M05 did not stop on these questions: each has a default that the branch ships, and the work went on with it. They are open until the owner answers.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — How many students a teacher shows in the graph

**Question:** In the demo data one hub teacher has 125 students. Drawn in full, the Lineage view is one row thousands of pixels wide: the people around the focus are a few pixels tall at the zoom that fits the whole view, and the layout worker takes longer. Plan §4.3 and §4.8 do not say how many students of one teacher the explorer draws. How many should it draw?

**Options:**
1. **12 per teacher, the rest behind a "+N students" chip** (the shipped default, `FANOUT_SHOWN` in `src/components/lineage/explorer/state.ts`): verified first, then display-primary, then those with more students, then by name. The focus's links to its own teachers are always drawn. Everyone stays in the List view. A view of more than 40 people opens centred on the focus with full cards (zoom 1; 0.75 on a phone) rather than fitted whole, where everyone would be a dot; "Fit the whole view" shows all of it. The profile page (M04) also lists 12 students.
2. **Everyone,** with the first fit on the focus only.
3. **Another number** of your choice.

**Proposed default:** option 1. M06 (expanding a node) can then open the rest of a teacher's students in place.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — The JavaScript budget of /explore

**Question:** Plan §5.8 (c) gives the explorer its own JavaScript budget, recorded in M05. Measured on 2026-10-25, `/explore` loads 191.4 kB (gzip) initially: the shared app code (about 162 kB, as on every page) plus the client island with TanStack Query, the oRPC client and nuqs, which In scope 1 requires. React Flow (58.4 kB) loads after the page with `next/dynamic`, and ELK (433.8 kB) only in the layout worker; neither is counted. Is 200 kB the right budget?

**Options:**
1. **200 kB** (the shipped default, `ROUTE_BUDGETS['/explore']` in `scripts/check-bundle-budget.mjs`, ADR 0009): 8.6 kB of room above the measurement.
2. **170 kB, the content-route default:** would need dropping one of the libraries In scope 1 names, or moving the toolbar out of the first load.
3. **Another number.**

**Proposed default:** option 1.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — The title on a full card, and compact cards on a phone

**Question:** Plan §4.8 lists a full card as "monogram, name, title, badges", and §4.3 says a phone starts with compact nodes. The data model has no title field for a person (`lineageNodeSchema` in `src/server/dto/public.ts`, the `person` table), and CLAUDE.md §7 forbids inventing ranks or titles. At 390 px the compact chip (120×36) drops the generation label, the status word and the "2 teachers" chip, which the screens review found worse than full cards at a smaller zoom. What should M05 do?

**Options:**
1. **No title until the data model has one; phones start at the zoom that fits the view, with full cards whenever that zoom is 0.75 or more** (the shipped default). The full card has the monogram (initials of the Latin name), the name, the original script, the generation line, the status seal with its word and the chips. A large view opens at 0.75 on a phone, the smallest zoom with full cards.
2. **Add a title field** to the person record (a migration, the public DTO and the edit forms of M09) and show it on the card.
3. **Phones start with compact nodes** as §4.3 says, whatever the size of the view.

**Proposed default:** option 1; option 2 belongs with the profile editing of M09 if titles are wanted.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — React Flow outside the /explore budget

**Question:** The route budget counts the JavaScript a page loads before it is interactive. The explorer's canvas (React Flow and `canvas.tsx`, 58.4 kB gzip) loads right after with `next/dynamic`, and `scripts/check-bundle-budget.mjs` does not gate that chunk; the milestone excludes only ELK. Should the canvas chunk get a gate of its own?

**Options:**
1. **Record it in ADR 0009 and gate it later** (the shipped default): the measured size is in the ADR; a gate for lazy chunks is added with the map (M11), which has the same shape.
2. **Gate it now:** extend the budget script to read the dynamic chunks of `/explore` and fail above, for example, 70 kB.

**Proposed default:** option 1.
