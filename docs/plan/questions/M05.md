# M05 — questions for the owner

M05 did not stop on these questions: each has a default that the branch ships, and the work went on with it. They are open until the owner answers.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — How many students a teacher shows in the graph

**Question:** In the demo data one hub teacher has 125 students. Drawn in full, the Lineage view is one row thousands of pixels wide: the people around the focus are a few pixels tall at the zoom that fits the whole view, and the layout worker takes longer. Plan §4.3 and §4.8 do not say how many students of one teacher the explorer draws. How many should it draw?

**Options:**
1. **12 per teacher, the rest behind a "+N students" chip** (the shipped default, `FANOUT_SHOWN` in `src/components/lineage/explorer/state.ts`): verified first, then active (training not ended), then display-primary, then those with more students, then by name; the chip counts every public student not drawn, including those the ego read's fan-out limit left out. The focus's links to its own teachers are always drawn. Everyone stays in the List view. A view of more than 40 drawn people opens on the focus with as many of its drawn teachers and students as fit beside it with full cards (zoom 0.75–1; 0.75 on a phone), nearest first, rather than fitted whole, where everyone would be a dot; "Fit the whole view" shows all of it. The profile page (M04) also lists 12 students. (Update 2026-10-26: the rule is now the zoom of the whole fit, not the number of people. A view that the pane shows whole only below the compact card's zoom (0.4, `READABLE_ZOOM` in `src/components/lineage/explorer/canvas.tsx`), where everyone would be a dot without a name, opens on the focus as above, on any screen. On a phone that includes a teacher with twelve students in one row, such as the Person view of demo-hub-a, which the 40-people rule opened at zoom 0.13. ADR 0009 item 5.)
2. **Everyone,** with the first fit on the focus only.
3. **Another number** of your choice.

**Proposed default:** option 1. M06 (expanding a node) can then open the rest of a teacher's students in place.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — The JavaScript budget of /explore

**Question:** Plan §5.8 (c) gives the explorer its own JavaScript budget, recorded in M05. Measured on 2026-10-25, `/explore` loads 191.4 kB (gzip) initially: the shared app code (about 162 kB, as on every page) plus the client island with TanStack Query, the oRPC client and nuqs, which In scope 1 requires. React Flow (58.4 kB) loads after the page with `next/dynamic`, and ELK (433.8 kB) only in the layout worker; neither is counted. Is 200 kB the right budget?

**Options:**
1. **200 kB** (the shipped default, `ROUTE_BUDGETS['/explore']` in `scripts/check-bundle-budget.mjs`, ADR 0009): 8.6 kB of room above the measurement (6.7 kB after the cloud review fixes: 193.3 kB on 2026-10-26).
2. **170 kB, the content-route default:** would need dropping one of the libraries In scope 1 names, or moving the toolbar out of the first load.
3. **Another number.**

**Proposed default:** option 1.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — The title on a full card, and compact cards on a phone

**Question:** Plan §4.8 lists a full card as "monogram, name, title, badges", and §4.3 says a phone starts with compact nodes. The data model has no title field for a person (`lineageNodeSchema` in `src/server/dto/public.ts`, the `person` table), and CLAUDE.md §7 forbids inventing ranks or titles. At 390 px the compact chip (120×36) drops the generation label, the status word and the "2 teachers" chip, which the screens review found worse than full cards at a smaller zoom. What should M05 do?

**Options:**
1. **No title until the data model has one; phones start at the zoom that fits the view, with full cards whenever that zoom is 0.75 or more** (the shipped default). The full card has the monogram (initials of the Latin name), the name, the original script, the generation line, the status seal with its word and the chips. A large view opens at 0.75 on a phone, the smallest zoom with full cards. (Update 2026-10-26: a view that would fit only below 0.4, as dots, opens like a large view, also on a phone; see the first question.)
2. **Add a title field** to the person record (a migration, the public DTO and the edit forms of M09) and show it on the card.
3. **Phones start with compact nodes** as §4.3 says, whatever the size of the view.

**Proposed default:** option 1; option 2 belongs with the profile editing of M09 if titles are wanted.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — A student whose teachers both stand on the focus spine

**Question:** Plan §4.6 says a student with two teachers stands horizontally between them, "including exactly under the preferred one". Plan §4.5 and M03 keep the focus spine (the chain of preferred teachers from the focus upwards) a straight vertical line through the centres of its cards. Sometimes all of a student's teachers in one lineage stand on that spine: a teacher and that teacher's own teacher, one above the other. Then "between them" is a single x, and only one card fits there in each row. If the place on the line in the student's row is already taken, by a spine card in the same row, by a spine edge passing through the row, or by another student of the same teachers, the student cannot stand between its teachers unless the spine bends.

In the demo data this happens in every ego view of demo-hub-b (Elena Notreal and Law Chui Sing, students of both demo-hub-b and demo-root-b) and in the full ego view of demo-hub-a in fixtures/lineage-600.json. The synthetic test data also has the spine-card variant. Every other such student now stands between its teachers in every view the tests cover, including the ego view of every person in both datasets, with and without the fan-out cap. What should the layout do in this case?

**Options:**
1. **Keep the spine straight; the student stands beside the line** (the shipped default, `src/lib/lineage-layout/converging.ts`, ADR 0005 item 10). When the place on the line is free, the first such student in §4.5 order takes it; any other (every one, when a spine card or a spine edge holds the place) stands beside the line on the side where the first layout pass put it, usually one card step away (224 px at the explorer's spacing); the distance is not bounded (up to 448 px in random test graphs). The layout reports the student in `LayoutStats.unplacedStudents`. `converging.test.ts` allows at most one such student in each view it checks, requires each to be reported in `LayoutStats.unplacedStudents` and checks that the spine has no bend; it does not bound the distance from the line.
2. **Bend the spine between the two teachers** so that the student fits between them. This breaks the M03 rule "focus spine without bend points" (layout.test.ts, `pnpm bench:layout`, scripts/verify.d/M03.sh).
3. **Change the wording of §4.6.** "Between" would read as "between, or directly beside the line when all the teachers stand one above the other on the focus spine". The layout stays as in option 1, and the plan text says so.

**Proposed default:** option 1. Option 3 is the same layout with the plan text made explicit.
