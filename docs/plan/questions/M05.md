M05 did not stop on these questions: each has a default that the branch ships, and the work went on with it. They are open until the owner answers.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — How many students a teacher shows in the graph

**Question:** In the demo data one hub teacher has 125 students. Drawn in full, the Lineage view is one row thousands of pixels wide: the people around the focus are a few pixels tall at the zoom that fits the whole view, and the layout worker takes longer. Plan §4.3 and §4.8 do not say how many students of one teacher the explorer draws. How many should it draw?

**Options:**
1. **12 per teacher, the rest behind a "+N students" chip** (the shipped default, `FANOUT_SHOWN` in `src/components/lineage/explorer/state.ts`): verified first, then display-primary, then those with more students, then by name. The focus's links to its own teachers are always drawn. Everyone stays in the List view. A view of more than 40 people opens centred on the focus at full-card zoom (0.75) rather than fitted whole, where everyone would be a dot; "Fit the whole view" shows all of it. The profile page (M04) also lists 12 students.
2. **Everyone,** with the first fit on the focus only.
3. **Another number** of your choice.

**Proposed default:** option 1. M06 (expanding a node) can then open the rest of a teacher's students in place.

## 2026-10-25 — M05 — The JavaScript budget of /explore

**Question:** Plan §5.8 (c) gives the explorer its own JavaScript budget, recorded in M05. Measured on 2026-10-25, `/explore` loads 190.3 kB (gzip) initially: the shared app code (about 162 kB, as on every page) plus the client island with TanStack Query, the oRPC client and nuqs, which In scope 1 requires. React Flow (58.4 kB) loads after the page with `next/dynamic`, and ELK (433.8 kB) only in the layout worker; neither is counted. Is 200 kB the right budget?

**Options:**
1. **200 kB** (the shipped default, `ROUTE_BUDGETS['/explore']` in `scripts/check-bundle-budget.mjs`, ADR 0009): 9.7 kB of room above the measurement.
2. **170 kB, the content-route default:** would need dropping one of the libraries In scope 1 names, or moving the toolbar out of the first load.
3. **Another number.**

**Proposed default:** option 1.
