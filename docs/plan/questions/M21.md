# M21 — questions for the owner

M21 did not stop on these questions. Each one keeps its proposed default until the owner answers it. The defaults are marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT M21`, with the question's number, and docs/adr/0024-trial-lessons.md).

## 2026-11-10 — M21 — 1. The shape of the schedule and the fees

**Question:** What does a school's schedule hold, and are the schedule and fees behind a plan?

**Options:**
1. **Weekly rows only. Each row is a weekday, a start and an end as wall-clock times in the zone of the school's city (`place.timezone`; UTC when the extract names none) and a short label (at most 60 characters). A schedule has at most 30 rows. The fees are one free text of at most 500 characters, plus a "the first trial lesson is free" flag. Every listed school has them, whatever its plan.**
2. Structured fees (amount, currency, period) as well as the free text.
3. Schedule and fees only on Professional Sifu.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied. The limits are `SCHOOL_SCHEDULE_MAX`, `SCHOOL_SCHEDULE_LABEL_MAX` and `SCHOOL_FEES_MAX` in `src/server/db/schema/common.ts`; the service is `src/server/services/school-schedule.ts`. The public page shows the schedule and fees while the school is public (`v_public_school`).

## 2026-11-10 — M21 — 2. A schedule edit and moderation

**Question:** Does a change of the schedule or the fees reopen the moderators' review of a listed school?

**Options:**
1. **No, like the description, the contacts and the forms taught. The listing stays listed and `updated_at` moves (a reviewer holding an older version reloads it). The audit row `school.schedule_update` names the fields that changed, never their text. A moderator can still hide the listing. The edit counts as activity (M20, D17): `last_activity_at` moves and the activity check starts again.**
2. Yes: every schedule change goes back to review.
3. A change of the schedule is not activity.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`updateSchoolSchedule`, `ACTIVITY_RESET`). An edit that saves the same values changes nothing and is not activity.

## 2026-11-10 — M21 — 3. Who may send a trial lesson request, and the limits

**Question:** Who may ask a school for a trial lesson, and how often?

**Options:**
1. **A signed-in account with a confirmed e-mail address, the current sign-up confirmations (18 or older among them; Phase 1 is for adults) and a profile, whose primary name the school sees (a copy taken when the request is sent). Only at a school `v_public_school` shows, never at the account's own school. One open request per sender and school (a partial unique index, `trial_inquiry_one_open_key`), at most 5 in 24 hours across schools. The preferred times and the message (at most 1000 characters) are optional. A visitor without a session gets "Sign in to request a trial lesson", which comes back to the form (`?next=`).**
2. Without an account, with an e-mail address typed into the form.
3. Other limits (for example 3 a day, or several open requests per school).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`TRIAL_DAILY_LIMIT` in `common.ts`; `refusalOf` in `src/server/services/trial-lessons.ts`). A request over the limit gets 429, an unconfirmed account 403, and the other refusals 409. Staff accounts have no profile, so they cannot send one.

## 2026-11-10 — M21 — 4. Who reads a request, and who is told

**Question:** Who sees a request with the sender's name and contact, and who gets which notice?

**Options:**
1. **The sender, the school's listing account, and the admins of the active organizations whose link the school accepted. Staff do not read requests (404): this is private contact between two people, and a report goes through the existing report path. A new request tells the owner and those admins (`school.trial-requested`). An answer (with the reply) and a decline (with the reason, if given) tell the sender (`school.trial-answered`, `school.trial-declined`). A close tells nobody. All are in the `schools` category: in the dashboard always, by e-mail unless switched off. Close works from open or answered; answer and decline only from open.**
2. Staff with a second factor can read requests too.
3. Only the listing account, not the organization admins.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`schoolHandler`, `decide`). Notices, audit rows and logs carry ids and states only, never a name, contact or message.

## 2026-11-10 — M21 — 5. How long requests are kept

**Question:** When is a request with the sender's contact deleted?

**Options:**
1. **365 days after it closed (answered, declined or closed), by the existing daily `retention.purge` job. An open request is kept until it is handled.**
2. 90 days after it closed.
3. Also delete open requests after a time (for example 180 days without an answer).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`TRIAL_INQUIRY_RETENTION_DAYS`, `purgeOldTrialData` in `src/server/services/retention.ts`). Deleting the sender's account or the school deletes its requests (`ON DELETE CASCADE`).

## 2026-11-10 — M21 — 6. Which plan the statistics need

**Question:** Which entitlement gates the school statistics, and what does a Free Sifu see?

**Options:**
1. **The existing row `school_business` of specification §3.1 ("School schedule and business profile": Free Sifu "No", Professional Sifu "Yes"), now `availability: 'now'`. It gates the statistics only: the schedule, the fees and the trial requests stay on every plan. On Free Sifu the statistics page shows the calm UpgradePrompt and no numbers (the counters are not read at all). `PLAN_MATRIX_VERSION` becomes `2026-11-10.1`.**
2. A new matrix row "School statistics" (it would change the plan table of the specification).
3. Statistics on every plan.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/entitlements/plans.ts`; `statsOffer` in `src/server/services/school-stats.ts`). The counters are kept on every plan, so an upgrade shows past weeks at once.

## 2026-11-10 — M21 — 7. What the statistics count

**Question:** What is counted, and what is left out?

**Options:**
1. **Counters per school and UTC day: views of the public page, openings of the school's pin or card in the finder, clicks on its website, e-mail or phone, and trial requests. Only numbers; no IP, cookie, account or fingerprint, so no unique visitors. Left out: the school's own listing account, staff (moderator, archivist, admin) and user agents that look like bots, headless browsers among them (`src/lib/bots.ts`). Page, pin and contact events arrive through a beacon to `POST /api/school-stats` (the same procedure as `POST /api/v1/schools/track`), which always answers 204. Trial requests are counted on the server, unless the sender is staff. The owner sees 12 ISO weeks (Monday, UTC). Counters older than 400 days are deleted by `retention.purge`.**
2. Unique visitors through a daily salted hash of the address.
3. A longer or shorter history.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`SCHOOL_STATS_WEEKS`, `SCHOOL_STATS_RETENTION_DAYS` in `common.ts`; `trackSchoolEvent`, `schoolStats`). A user agent can be faked, so the counts are an estimate, and the page says "Counts only".
