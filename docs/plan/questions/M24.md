# M24 — questions for the owner

M24 did not stop on these questions. Each one keeps its proposed default until the owner answers it. The defaults are marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT M24`, with the question's number, and docs/adr/0027-organization-tools.md).

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 1. The file format of the bulk import

**Question:** Which files does the bulk import of specification §9.1 accept?

**Options:**
1. **CSV only: UTF-8 (with or without a byte order mark), comma or semicolon separated (the separator is read from the header line), quoted fields as RFC 4180. Excel users save as "CSV UTF-8". The parser is plain TypeScript in `src/server/services/organization-import-csv.ts`; no new dependency.**
2. CSV and Excel (`.xlsx`), which needs a spreadsheet library (a new dependency with a large attack surface).
3. A form in the app that takes one row at a time.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied. A file that is not UTF-8, has no First_Name column or has no rows is refused as a whole with its reason.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 2. Limits of an import

**Question:** How large may a file be, how often may an organization check one, and how long does a preview stay open?

**Options:**
1. **At most 500 rows and 256 KB per file; at most 10 previews per organization in 24 hours (`TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` after that); a preview can be imported for 24 hours, then it expires, its parsed fields are cleared and nothing is imported. Expired previews are closed when they are next read and by the daily `retention.purge` job.**
2. Larger files (5,000 rows) imported in a background job.
3. No daily allowance.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`ORGANIZATION_IMPORT_*` in `src/server/db/schema/organization-tools.ts`). A larger federation splits its file.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 3. The Email column

**Question:** The template of specification §9.1 has an Email column. What does the app do with it?

**Options:**
1. **Nothing: the column is accepted so the template stays the same, but its values are never read, stored, shown or logged. An import invites and contacts no one. A member who wants an account signs up and asks for the record through the existing paths.**
2. Store the address on the record, private to the organization.
3. Send each address an invitation to claim the record.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied. Options 2 and 3 would hold or use personal data of people who never agreed to it (plan §6.4) and would let an organization trigger mail to anyone.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 4. Imported teacher links

**Question:** What is a teacher named in Sifu_Database_ID?

**Options:**
1. **An `organization_teacher_claim`: "Claimed by the organization, not verified". It is never a `teaching_relationship`, never public, never in the lineage, and never turns into one by itself. A verified relationship needs the path of M09: the student (once the record has an account) asks the teacher and the teacher approves. The organization can withdraw its claim; separation withdraws it too.**
2. A `teaching_relationship` waiting for the teacher's approval (`pending_teacher`).
3. A verified relationship, because the organization vouches for it.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (DEFAULT D12). Option 3 would let an import bypass the teacher's approval, which the Phase 3 completion condition forbids; option 2 would put requests in a teacher's queue that no student made.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 5. Matching imported rows with existing people

**Question:** Is an imported row ever matched or merged with an existing person or account?

**Options:**
1. **Never. Each valid row creates a new private record (unclaimed, `involved_only`, no account). A row whose name equals a current member of the same organization is refused as a likely duplicate, and so is a repeated name inside the file. Nothing outside the organization is ever compared, so a file never learns whether a person of another organization exists. The teacher must be a public profile or a current member of the same organization.**
2. Match by name and e-mail address with existing accounts and link the record to them.
3. Offer possible matches for the administrator to confirm.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied. Options 2 and 3 would let an organization find out or take over other people's records.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 6. The script of Chinese_Name

**Question:** How is the Chinese_Name column stored?

**Options:**
1. **As a non-primary name in Traditional Chinese (`lang = zh-Hant`, script `Hant`), next to the Latin primary name from First_Name and Last_Name. The person's own editor can change it later.**
2. Detect Traditional or Simplified per row.
3. Ask for the script in another column.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied. Search finds the person by every spelling either way (M07).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 7. The heatmap's grid and smallest square

**Question:** How coarse is the heatmap, so that no school can be located?

**Options:**
1. **Squares of 1° of latitude and longitude; a school counts at its city's centre (`place.centroid`), never at its own point (the service never reads `school.public_point` or `school_private_location`). A square with fewer than 3 schools is not drawn; its schools are counted by country only. The answer carries the south-west corner, the size, the count and the countries of each square.**
2. Squares of 0.5° with a minimum of 2.
3. Counts by city only, without a map.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`HEATMAP_CELL_DEGREES`, `HEATMAP_MIN_SCHOOLS` in `src/server/services/organization-insights.ts`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 8. What the analytics count, and who sees them

**Question:** What do the organization's analytics show, and are they a plan feature?

**Options:**
1. **Counts of the organization's own records only (members, schools, claims, by country, city and month), and of the verification of current members as students in public states only (verified, in arbitration; DEFAULT D03), so a member's private pending request is never counted. Analytics and the heatmap are Federation features (specification §10.3, DEFAULT D08): an approved organization without the plan sees what the page needs. Only the organization's admins see them.**
2. Analytics for every approved organization, the heatmap with Federation.
3. Include pending requests.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied. The plan matrix and `PLAN_MATRIX_VERSION` do not change; Federation stays planned in the public catalogue and is given as a test grant.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 9. Separation: who decides, what goes and what stays

**Question:** Specification §10.3 asks for separation rules. What happens when a member and an organization part?

**Options:**
1. **Either side alone, without approval: the member leaves (`organizationTools.leave`, no plan needed) or the organization ends the membership (`organizations.endMember`). What goes: the affiliation (ends today), the organization's own record of the member (rank and city) and its open teacher claims about the member, as the student or as the teacher (withdrawn, reason `separation`). In the organization's lists a claimed person is named only while they are a public profile or a current member. What stays: the membership history, the person and profile, every teaching relationship and the lineage, the school and its pin, the member's own plans.**
2. Leaving needs the organization's approval.
3. The organization keeps its record of the former member.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/services/organization-separation-core.ts`). The rules are shown on the member's and the organization's dashboards. The 90-day independence of a school after non-payment (§10.3) belongs to billing and is not part of M24.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M24 — 10. Who is told about a separation

**Question:** Which notices does a separation send?

**Options:**
1. **In the dashboard only: the organization's admins when a member leaves (`organization.member_left`), the member's account when the organization ends the membership (`organization.membership_ended`). The end of a school link keeps its notice from M15. No e-mail.**
2. E-mail as well.
3. No notice.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/templates/notifications.ts`, the `organizations` category).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)
