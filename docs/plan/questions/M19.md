# M19 — questions for the owner

M19 did not stop on these questions: each keeps its proposed default, marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT M19 (question N)` and docs/adr/0022-passport.md), until the owner answers it.

## 2026-11-07 — M19 — Who writes the entries of a passport

**Question:** Who may record a rank, an examination or a form or skill in a person's passport, and what may the person do with their own?

**Options:**
1. **The teacher with a `verified` relationship to the student (with any approved organization named as the issuer), or an admin of an approved organization for a current member of it. A relationship `in_arbitration` reads only. The student never records their own; they make an entry private or public, propose a correction (whoever recorded it is told and changes it, or not) and upload certificates. Attendance at an event is marked by its organizer or an instructor, after it took place, for their own verified students only. Whoever recorded an entry changes or withdraws it (kept, shown to nobody); staff with a verified second factor remove any entry with a reason (audited, the owner told).**
2. The same, and the student may also record a rank, shown as "self-declared" until a teacher confirms it.
3. Only the organizations record ranks and examinations; teachers record forms and skills.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`writerFor`, `mayChange` and `updateEntry` in `src/server/services/passport.ts`; the refusal `STUDENT_CANNOT_RECORD`, `ARBITRATION_READ_ONLY`). A teacher may name any approved organization as the issuer of what they record; an admin records only for members of the organization they administer. Anyone else gets the answer of a student that is not there (404).

## 2026-11-07 — M19 — How precise experience and dates are

**Question:** Dates are often remembered only as a year. How does the passport show the length of training and the dates of entries?

**Options:**
1. **Every date keeps its precision (`year`, `month`, `day` or `unknown`) and is shown as "2019", "May 2019" or "12 May 2019". Experience runs from the earliest start (the teachers' training periods of M09 and the year training began the person gave) to today while a period goes on, otherwise to the latest end. A year anywhere among the dates it needs gives whole years with "about" ("since 2015 · about 10 years"), never months; months only when every date it needs is known to the month ("since March 2015 · 10 years, 7 months"). Within one year at year precision only "since 2026".**
2. Always months, counting a year-only date from January.
3. Only the start year, never a length.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`experience()` in `src/lib/passport-time.ts`, unit-tested in `passport-time.test.ts`). A date after today or before 1900 is refused. The timeline sorts a year-only entry after the finer ones of its year (it could be any time in it); undated entries come last.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-07 — M19 — What Free and Premium show

**Question:** What does the public profile show of a passport on each plan, and what happens on a downgrade?

**Options:**
1. **`passport` is available now and enforced. The basic passport (Free Student, Free Sifu, and a record without an account) shows the current rank (the latest public one by date), the teachers with their periods and the year training began. The full passport (Premium Student, Professional Sifu, Federation) shows also the timeline: every public examination, form or skill, certificate and event. On a downgrade nothing is deleted (D08): the entries are kept and hidden from the profile, the owner sees them on /dashboard/passport marked "Public, kept", with the upgrade prompt of M08 (only benefits available now). Recording for students is not limited by plan in the pilot.**
2. The basic passport shows also the examinations (without certificates and events).
3. Every plan shows the full passport (no gating).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`passportLevel` and `FULL_PASSPORT_PLANS` in `src/server/entitlements/plans.ts`, `PLAN_MATRIX_VERSION` 2026-11-07.1; `passportGet` and `myPassport` in `src/server/services/passport.ts`). The plan is that of the passport's own account, so a visitor's plan changes nothing.

## 2026-11-07 — M19 — Scanned certificates

**Question:** What may a student upload as a certificate, how many, and how is it served?

**Options:**
1. **Images only (JPEG, PNG, WebP; no PDF in the pilot), at most 5 MB, at most 20 active certificates per passport, processed like gallery photos (encoded again, without EXIF, GPS, XMP or IPTC; squares of 96 and 512 px and the whole image). Every file is in the private store (the evidence bucket, never the photo bucket), read only through the app at `/api/certificates/<id>/<hash>/<size>.webp`, never by a presigned address. A private certificate is served only to the student and their teachers (an established relationship), `private, no-store`; a public one that the profile shows (public person, full passport) to everyone, `public, no-cache`; everyone else gets the same 404. It is a scanned document, not a certificate the platform issues: no serial number, QR code or status page (D21, Phase 4).**
2. Also PDF, with a PDF library to render a preview (needs a dependency and a Stop and ask).
3. Public certificates in the photo bucket, served like gallery photos.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`uploadCertificate`, `openCertificate`, `PASSPORT_CERTIFICATES_MAX` in `src/server/db/schema/common.ts`; `src/app/api/certificates/[id]/[hash]/[file]/route.ts`).

## 2026-11-07 — M19 — The visibility a new entry starts with

**Question:** Is a new entry public or private until the student chooses?

**Options:**
1. **An entry a teacher or an organization records starts public (the student is told and can make it private at once); a certificate the student uploads starts private unless they choose public on the form.**
2. Every entry starts private until the student makes it public.
3. Every entry starts public.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (the column defaults in `src/server/db/schema/passport.ts`; `certificateUploadInput` in the service). A private entry is never in a public answer, the public view or a cache.

## 2026-11-07 — M19 — Choosing the examiner

**Question:** The examiner of an examination is a person of the archive. Whom does the form offer?

**Options:**
1. **The teacher themself, the teacher's own verified teachers and the student's other verified teachers (people the teacher knows on the record), or none. The API accepts any person of the archive; the profile names the examiner only while their own profile is public, and never otherwise.**
2. A search over the whole archive (the search palette of M07) in the form.
3. A free-text name, not linked to a profile.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`passportStudents` gives the choices; `cleanExaminer` checks the person; the public view `v_public_passport` joins the examiner through `v_public_person` only).
