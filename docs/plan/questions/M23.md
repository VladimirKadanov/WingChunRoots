# M23 — questions for the owner

M23 did not stop on these questions. Each one keeps its proposed default until the owner answers it. The defaults are marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT M23`, with the question's number, and docs/adr/0026-federation-billing.md).

## 2026-11-11 — M23 — 1. Who buys Federation, and what is sold

**Question:** Who may start, change and cancel a Federation subscription, and how is Federation sold while the plan matrix still lists it as planned?

**Options:**
1. **An owner of an approved organization of the kind `federation` buys, changes and cancels the subscription for the organization (the organization is the subject, never an individual). Another admin of the organization reads the billing page and the payment history but sees no form; the API answers 403 to its change. Any other account gets 404. Federation stays "Planned" on /plans as a whole (its planned features stay planned); it is sold, at the proposed prices of the matrix ($99 a month, $990 a year; DEFAULT D09, "Proposed price (not final)"), for what it gives now, which the billing page lists: members, linked schools and events published as the organization (`ORGANIZATION_PURCHASABLE_PLANS`, `FEDERATION_BENEFITS_NOW`; CLAUDE.md §11). An association or a national body cannot buy it.**
2. Every admin of the organization may buy and change.
3. Federation is sold only when the matrix marks it `now`.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`startCheckout`, `ownSubscription` and `organizationBilling` in `src/server/services/billing.ts`; `src/server/entitlements/plans.ts`; `/dashboard/organization/[id]/billing`).

## 2026-11-11 — M23 — 2. Switching between paid plans and billing periods

**Question:** When does a change of plan or billing period take effect, and how is it charged?

**Options:**
1. **An upgrade (a higher plan of the same audience, or monthly to yearly billing) takes effect now: a new period starts today and the unused part of the current one is credited against the new price (proration, invoiced at once). A downgrade (yearly to monthly, or a lower plan) is scheduled for the end of the period: the plan and its access stay until then, the renewal bills the new price, and the change can be taken back until then ("Keep the current plan and billing"). A cancelled or past-due subscription cannot change. During the free period of a trial that continued as a subscription, every change waits for that period's end. The Stripe adapter applies changes now (`proration_behavior=always_invoice`, `billing_cycle_anchor=now`); a change at the period end would need a Stripe subscription schedule, which it does not create yet, so it answers "cannot make this change" there (the test provider schedules it).**
2. Every change at the period end.
3. Every change now, with proration.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`changeTiming`, `changeSubscriptionPlan`, `cancelPlanChange` in `src/server/services/billing.ts`; `changePlan` and `cancelScheduledChange` of both providers). Today only Federation has a yearly price, so in practice the changes are an organization's; Premium Student and Professional Sifu belong to different account types and do not switch into each other.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M23 — 3. Subscribing during a trial

**Question:** What happens when the owner subscribes while the organization's trial runs?

**Options:**
1. **The subscription starts now, and its first period ends when the trial would have ended; that first invoice is $0.00, and the first payment of the chosen price is due at the trial's end. The trial becomes "Continued as a subscription"; its complimentary grant ends now and the subscription's own grant carries Federation on without a gap. The test checkout says "Nothing is charged today" and its button reads "Subscribe with the test card". With Stripe, the checkout sets `trial_end`.**
2. The subscription starts when the trial ends (a scheduled checkout).
3. The trial ends at once and the first period is charged today.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`startCheckout`, `convertTrial` in `src/server/services/billing.ts`; `pay` of the test provider; `createCheckout` of the Stripe adapter).

## 2026-11-11 — M23 — 4. Setting up a trial

**Question:** Who sets up a Federation trial, for whom, and for how long?

**Options:**
1. **An administrator with the second factor, on `/admin/organizations/<id>` (or `POST /api/v1/admin/organizations/trial/start`), with a reason kept in the audit log. Only for an approved federation that has no Federation plan now or scheduled, no live subscription and no earlier trial (one trial per organization). 6 or 12 months; 12 by default (specification §10.2). No card is asked for. The trial is a complimentary `entitlement_grant` from now to its end, linked from `federation_trial`. An administrator can end it early with a reason; the grant is revoked, nothing is deleted. If the grant is revoked directly on the grants page instead, the daily run marks the trial as ended early too.**
2. The owner starts the trial from the billing page.
3. Trials of any length.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/services/federation-trials.ts`; `/admin/organizations/[id]`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M23 — 5. The trial's notices, and who hears of an organization's billing

**Question:** Which notices does a trial send, to whom, and in which category?

**Options:**
1. **To every current admin of the organization: when the trial starts, 14 days and 7 days before it ends (only the latest step that is due, once per trial and step, so a late start or a missed day never sends both), and when it ends (by itself or early). Category Plan of M18: e-mail on by default, an admin may switch it off; no countdown or urgency, one sentence with a date. The billing notices of an organization's subscription (started, cancelled, payment failed, plan changed, change scheduled, ended) go to every current admin in the Billing category, which cannot be switched off, and link to the organization's billing page. A member school or student never hears of them.**
2. The owners only.
3. The billing category for the trial notices too.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/templates/catalog.ts`, `notifications.ts`; `runFederationTrials`, run daily by the job `plans.expiry`).

## 2026-11-11 — M23 — 6. Downstream independence

**Question:** What does the end of a federation's subscription or trial do to its member schools and students?

**Options:**
1. **Nothing (specification §10.3). A federation's subscription and trial create and end only grants of the organization; a school's Professional Sifu and a student's Premium Student are their own subscriptions or grants and never change with it. The members, the school links, the public pins and the organization's history all stay; only the organization's own Federation features stop until it has the plan again. Integration and E2E tests prove it for a cancelled subscription that ends, a lapsed one after the grace period, an expired trial and a trial ended early.**
2. Members lose a discount or a benefit the federation paid for (there is none today).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`syncGrant`, `convertTrial` in `src/server/services/billing.ts`; `src/server/services/federation-trials.ts`; `federation-billing.int.test.ts`, `e2e/m23-federation-billing.spec.ts`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)
