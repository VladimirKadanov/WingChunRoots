# M09 — questions for the owner

M09 did not stop on any of these questions. Each one keeps its proposed default, marked in the code, until the owner answers it.

## 2026-10-31 — M09 — Does a teacher edit the periods of a request?

**Question:** the milestone's stop-and-ask condition: when a student's request has the wrong dates, a wrong school or the wrong lineage, should the teacher correct it before approving, or only approve or decline?

**Options:**
1. **The student proposes; the teacher approves or declines with a neutral reason; the teacher never edits.** A declined request is corrected by the student and sent again (`relationships.resubmit`); the teacher gets it as a corrected request. Every version the teacher answers is the student's own words.
2. The teacher may edit the periods, the school and the lineage, and then approves the edited version; the student is told what changed.
3. The teacher may propose a correction, which the student accepts or rejects (a second round in both directions).

**Proposed default:** option 1, the milestone's own default (`src/server/services/relationships.ts`, `docs/adr/0011-teacher-requests.md`, `docs/guides/user-guide.bg.md`, „Учители и ученици: заявки и одобрения“). The neutral reasons are "The training dates need correcting.", "The lineage needs correcting.", "Some details need correcting. Check the request and send it again." and "I can’t confirm this training from the request as it was sent." (`src/server/templates/relationships.ts`). Option 2 or 3 would need an edit history per period and a second approval, and fits M10a's review queue better.

## 2026-10-31 — M09 — A plain form instead of TanStack Form for the request form

**Question:** plan §5.6 builds forms with TanStack Form. `/dashboard/profile` (M07) already sits at the edge of the 170 kB route budget because of it (`docs/plan/questions/M07.md`). Should `/dashboard/teachers/new` use it too?

**Options:**
1. **A plain form (native fields, `useActionState`, the account form components of M02) with the server's checks as the only rules.** The route stays within its budget; the errors come from `relationships.request` (the same as through `/api/v1`), shown in the error summary and at each field; it works without JavaScript.
2. TanStack Form with client-side Zod rules, as the profile editor: about 25 kB more on the route, over budget, so the budget would have to be raised (only the owner may).

**Proposed default:** option 1 (`src/app/dashboard/teachers/new/request-form.tsx`). The only rule worth checking before the server is "a start year of four digits", which the field's `inputMode` and `maxLength` already guide.

## 2026-10-31 — M09 — Requests to a teacher whose profile is not public, or who has no account

**Question:** the palette finds only public profiles (M07), and only a person with an account can answer. What happens with a teacher who is on the record but hidden, or who has no account yet?

**Options:**
1. **Only a public, living person with an account can be named in M09.** A hidden teacher gets "Choose your teacher from the search.", the same answer as for no such person (plan §6.4); a teacher without an account gets "Requests to teachers without an account open later in the pilot." Historical and missing teachers come with M10a (out of scope here).
2. Allow requests to hidden teachers by their exact profile address.

**Proposed default:** option 1 (`requestTeacher` in `src/server/services/relationships.ts`). Option 2 would let anyone learn that a hidden profile exists.
