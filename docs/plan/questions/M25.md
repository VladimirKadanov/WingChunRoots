# M25 — questions for the owner

M25 did not stop on these questions. Each one keeps its proposed default until the owner answers it. The defaults are marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT M25`, with the question's number, and docs/adr/0028-governance.md).

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 1. Who decides a succession request, and what it never does

**Question:** The specification (§15) asks for "a review by the board" before a successor takes over. Who is the board in Phase 1, and may a succession ever change a person's life status?

**Options:**
1. **The administrators (role `admin`, with the second factor) decide every succession request, after taking the case. No step of succession ever changes a person's life status: a reported death opens a review of the administration only; marking a record as deceased or memorial stays a separate archival decision (an archival edit or a record proposal).**
2. Any reviewer (moderator, archivist or administrator).
3. Two administrators must agree.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`DECIDERS.succession_request` in `src/server/services/review-core.ts`; `src/server/services/governance-review.ts`).

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 2. Who can be nominated

**Question:** The source says a successor is "a verified student with an active Tier 2 or Tier 3 profile". What does that mean here?

**Options:**
1. **A verified student of the owner's person (a verified teaching relationship, any visibility) who has an account and holds Premium Student or Professional Sifu now. The owner needs the `successor` entitlement: Professional Sifu, or the owner of an active organization with Federation. One live (pending or accepted) nomination per owner.**
2. Any verified student with an account, whatever the plan.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`SUCCESSOR_PLANS` and `maySucceed` in `src/server/services/succession.ts`). The plan matrix marks `successor` as available now (`PLAN_MATRIX_VERSION` 2026-11-11.1).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 3. Who may appeal

**Question:** Who may appeal which decision?

**Options:**
1. **A relationship dispute: either of its two people, whatever the outcome. A lineage report, a succession request and a record proposal: the account that opened the case, and only a refusal (an approved request or an upheld report has nothing to appeal). Anyone else gets the same answer as for no such case.**
2. Anyone involved, whatever the outcome.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`appealRole` in `src/server/services/disputes.ts`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 4. The appeal window, and a second appeal

**Question:** How long after a decision can it be appealed, and can the decision made after an appeal be appealed again?

**Options:**
1. **30 days after the decision. One appeal per decision (a unique index on the decision's review event), decided by a decider of the appealed kind who is not the member of staff who decided it. An upheld appeal reopens the case for a new decision by someone else; that decision is final (no second appeal of the same case).**
2. No window; one appeal per decision, also after an appeal.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`APPEAL_WINDOW_DAYS` in `src/server/db/schema/common.ts`; `appealStateOf`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 5. Rate limits

**Question:** How many reports, appeals, requests and proposals may an account send?

**Options:**
1. **At most 5 a day of each kind per account (lineage reports, appeals, succession requests, record proposals), counted under the account's case lock; one open report per account and relationship; one pending proposal per account and record; one pending request per nomination.**
2. No daily limit, only the one-open rules.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`GOVERNANCE_DAILY_LIMIT`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 6. Notices

**Question:** In which categories are the new notices, and which can be switched off?

**Options:**
1. **Nomination, answer and revocation: the `teaching` category. A succession request and its decision: the `account` category, which cannot be switched off (the owner must hear of a request about their account at once). Decisions on reports, appeals and proposals: the `review` category. All are e-mailed.**
2. Everything in `review`.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/templates/catalog.ts`).

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 7. Evidence for a proposed record change

**Question:** Does a proposal to change a shared archival record need evidence?

**Options:**
1. **Only a change of the life status needs at least one file; every other change needs a reason, files optional.**
2. Every proposal needs evidence.
3. No proposal needs evidence.

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 8. What an approved succession transfers

**Question:** What passes to the successor?

**Options:**
1. **The owner's school listings (`school.created_by_user_id`) and every live organization administration of the owner (the successor gets the same role, an owner's role included; the owner's own rows are revoked). Work that belongs to a school or organization (trial requests, school link requests) follows it. Pending teacher requests to the owner's person stay with that person: the successor is not the historical teacher. The record, its life status, students, relationships and history are not touched. What moved is kept on the request (`transferred`) and in the audit log.**
2. The schools only.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`decideSuccession` in `src/server/services/governance-review.ts`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 9. How inactivity is measured

**Question:** What counts as "activity" for the 180 days?

**Options:**
1. **The last sign-in (`auth_user.last_active_at`, set when a session is created) or the newest session refresh, whichever is later. More than 180 whole days opens the inactivity basis; it is checked again at approval, so an owner who comes back stops the transfer. The owner is told of every request at once and can stop it by revoking the nomination.**
2. Any request of the account (more precise, but a write on every request).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`inactiveDaysOf` and `stillInactive` in `src/server/services/succession.ts`; the migration backfills `last_active_at`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 10. Who reads the change history of a record

**Question:** Is the change history of a shared archival record public?

**Options:**
1. **Yes, for a public shared record: the date, whether it was an archival edit or an approved proposal, and the names of the fields that changed. Never who made it; the reason only for staff with the second factor. The history is append-only (`app_rw` has SELECT and INSERT only).**
2. Staff only.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`recordHistory` in `src/server/services/stewardship.ts`).

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-11 — M25 — 11. The people of a reported relationship

**Question:** Do the two people of a reported relationship learn of a report?

**Options:**
1. **Not of the report itself: they are no parties of the lineage report and never learn who reported. Only when a moderator upholds it is the relationship set in arbitration (the existing dispute of M10b): then both are told, asked for evidence and may appeal its decision.**
2. They are told of every report.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`reportLineage` in `src/server/services/disputes.ts`).
