## 2026-10-25 — M07 — What an account deletion keeps

**Question:** `/account/privacy` lets a member ask for their account to be deleted. The request opens a `review_case` of kind `deletion_request` and notifies every administrator (audit `account.deletion_request`). When an administrator carries it out, what is kept?

**Options:**
1. **Delete the account's data; keep the relationships that others verified as historical records until the legal review.** The sign-in account, its sessions, its consents' link to a person, its profile names in their own script, biography, city and year are deleted; the person record stays as an unclaimed historical record with its Latin display name only, so the teachers' and students' verified relationships do not disappear from their lineages. The audit log keeps its rows (append-only, ids only, no names).
2. Delete everything, including the person record and every relationship it is part of. Other members lose the verified links to this person.
3. Keep the person record as it is, public, and delete only the sign-in account.

**Proposed default:** option 1 (`DEFAULT` in `src/server/services/privacy.ts`, `docs/guides/admin-guide.bg.md`). Until the review tool of M10a, `pnpm account:delete --case <id>` carries a request out in one transaction: the consent records are deleted too, and a profile that was public only by its owner's consent (no verified relationship) becomes private; `--cancel` closes a request the member withdrew. The legal review (D23) decides the final retention before real participants join.

## 2026-10-25 — M07 — Hiding a public profile that has verified relationships

**Question:** plan §6.2 makes a person public when one of its relationships becomes `verified`, or when the owner of a self-stewarded profile publishes it. M07's "Show my profile publicly" lets the owner untick that box, and withdrawing the `publication` consent on `/account/privacy` does the same. Should unticking also hide a profile that is public because of a verified relationship?

**Options:**
1. **Yes: the owner's choice wins.** A self-stewarded profile is public only while its owner keeps it so. While it is hidden, `v_public_edge` leaves out its relationships (both ends must be public), so it is also missing from its teachers' and students' public lineages.
2. No: a profile with a verified relationship stays public; the box only controls profiles without one. The owner can still ask for deletion.
3. Hide the profile's own page and search entry but keep the person, with the Latin display name only, as a node in other people's lineages.

**Proposed default:** option 1 (`DEFAULT D03` in `src/server/services/profile.ts`). It follows the consent the member gave or withdrew and never shows a profile its owner chose to hide; option 3 needs a new kind of public node and is left for a later milestone if the owner prefers it.

## 2026-10-25 — M07 — Route JavaScript budgets of /dashboard/profile and /styleguide

**Question:** `scripts/verify.sh all` (check `checks:M00b`, `scripts/check-bundle-budget.mjs`) fails on two routes, and neither can get under its budget without a change that only the owner may approve (CLAUDE.md §9: a budget is never raised to get a pass). How should they be handled?

Measured on the cloud build of this branch (gzip, initially loaded JavaScript, plan §5.8 (c)):

- **`/dashboard/profile`: 191.2 kB of 170 kB.** The shell and router that every route loads are about 150–155 kB; the profile editor adds TanStack Form (24.9 kB, required by M07 In scope 1 and plan §5.6) and its own code (10.5 kB). The Zod rules are no longer part of the page: the editor fetches them when the person first focuses the form or saves it (about 40 kB that Turbopack does not tree-shake, `zod/mini` included). An earlier fix in this branch wrapped the editor in `next/dynamic`; that only moved the editor's chunk out of the count while the browser still preloads and runs it with the page, so it was reverted.
- **`/styleguide`: 254.5 kB of 250 kB** (247.0 kB on `main`). The developer-only, noindex page renders every overlay at once. The search palette (loaded on first use) uses Base UI's dialog and floating modules, so Turbopack moves those into a chunk shared with the palette and loses scope hoisting for them: about 6 kB more gzip on this page. Every other route grew by about 1.5 kB (the search field in the shell) and stays within 170 kB.

**Options:**
1. **Give both routes their own budget, recorded as `DEFAULT` in `ROUTE_BUDGETS`:** `/dashboard/profile` 195 kB (a signed-in, noindex form page; TanStack Form is the cost of the milestone's own requirement) and `/styleguide` 260 kB (as M00b gave it its own 250 kB). Plan §5.8 (c) already lets routes have their own budgets.
2. Keep 170 kB and load TanStack Form on the first interaction: the page stays the plain HTML form (which already posts without JavaScript) until the person focuses it. More code, and text typed before the editor arrives must be carried over; for `/styleguide`, load the overlay demos when they are opened.
3. Keep 170 kB for the profile by writing the editor with plain React state instead of TanStack Form (a deviation from M07 In scope 1 and plan §5.6).

**Proposed default:** none is applied: both checks stay red until the owner answers. Option 1 is the recommendation; the change is the two `ROUTE_BUDGETS` lines and a note in ADR 0006.
