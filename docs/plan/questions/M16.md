## 2026-11-03 — M16 — How many room photos a school's gallery holds

**Question:** The owner decided that a school's room photos and its logo are a gallery of their own, separate from the person's plan allowance (`photos`, D07). How many room photos may one school keep?

**Options:**
1. **12 room photos per school, the same for every plan, and one logo besides them (the logo never counts). At the limit the upload is refused with a sentence; there is no upgrade prompt, because no plan raises it.**
2. A limit per plan of the account that listed the school (a new `school_photos` row in `src/server/entitlements/plans.ts`, with admin grants).
3. Another fixed number (6, 24, …).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`SCHOOL_ROOM_PHOTOS_MAX = 12` in `src/server/db/schema/common.ts`, marked `DEFAULT D07 (M16)`; `uploadSchoolPhoto` in `src/server/services/school-photos.ts`). Option 2 would add an entitlement row and a quota check under the school's lock; the stored rows need no change.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-03 — M16 — Two full cards side by side on a 390 px phone

**Question:** The owner asked for the card photo to be a square as high as the card on its leading edge, the card wider by that amount. The full card is now 264×64 (it was 200×64). React Flow's fit on a 390 px phone leaves (390 − 50) / 0.75 = 453 px for full cards; two columns of one card each need 2 × 264 + 24 + 2 × 12 = 576 px, so two full cards (two lineage columns, or a stacked block of two) no longer fit side by side at the zoom of full cards. Which way?

**Options:**
1. **Keep the 264 px card. A phone opens the view on the focus (or the root) with the people that fit at the zoom of full cards, nearest first (`largeViewStart`, unchanged), and the rest is reached by panning or zooming out to compact chips; the columns, ELK's settings, the stacks (ADR 0017) and the gaps stay as they are.**
2. A narrower text area on the card (for example 176 + 64 = 240 px: 2 × 240 + 48 = 528 px, still over 453), so the names are cut sooner and two cards still do not fit.
3. A smaller zoom of full cards on a phone (`DETAIL_ZOOM.full` 0.75 → 0.58), so two 264 px cards fit; the text on them is smaller than the design system's least size.
4. On a phone only, the photo moves inside the old 200 px card (the M14 round thumbnail), and the square shows only from 768 px.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`NODE_SIZES.full` in `src/lib/lineage-layout/presets.ts`; the comment of `COLUMN_GAP` in `src/lib/lineage-layout/scene.ts`). No test or check was weakened: the unit tests of the explorer's first views build their own 200 px slots, and the curve tests of `scene.test.ts` keep runs at least as long in px as before.

**Answer (owner, 2026-11-03 11:24, verbatim):** "about the phone side by side cards, reduce the whole card scale a bit to fit them only on phone".

**Applied (option 3, phones only):** the 264×64 card with its 64×64 square stays everywhere. On a pane narrower than 640 px (`NARROW_PANE`), full cards start at `PHONE_FULL_ZOOM` = 0.75 × (2 × 200 + 24) / (2 × 264 + 24) ≈ 0.576 instead of 0.75 (`fullZoomOf` in `src/components/lineage/explorer/canvas.tsx`), so two full cards and the column gap are as wide on screen as two 200 px cards were at 0.75 (318 px) and fit side by side at 390 px (and at 360 px) again. A phone's large view starts at that zoom. Desktops and tablets keep 0.75. The name on a full card on a phone is about 9 px on screen at that zoom (16 px × 0.576; it was 12 px), below the design system's least size: the owner's accepted trade-off; **Zoom in** makes it larger. The unit tests of a phone's first view whose geometry depends on the card's width now use the 264 px slot (`FULL` in `canvas.test.ts`), and a new test pins the phone threshold.
