# M17 — questions for the owner

M17 did not stop on these questions: each keeps its proposed default, marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT M17 (question N)`, `DEFAULT D25` and docs/adr/0020-events.md), until the owner answers it.

## 2026-11-04 — M17 — How the monthly allowance of Professional Sifu is counted

**Question:** The specification gives Professional Sifu "up to 2 events per month". Which events count, and in which month?

**Options:**
1. **The published events (not the cancelled and not the removed ones) that start in one calendar month, the month taken in the event's own time zone. Moving an event to another month checks that month again. The count and the write share the account's quota lock (`lockQuota`, READ COMMITTED), so two publications at once never pass the limit together.**
2. The events published (created) in a calendar month, whatever their dates.
3. A rolling window (any 30 days).
4. The month in UTC, or in the organizer's zone.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`checkMonth` and `publishedInMonth` in `src/server/services/events.ts`, `monthKey` in `src/lib/event-time.ts`; marked `DEFAULT M17 (question 1)`). A cancelled event frees its place. The dashboard shows each month that has events with "1 of 2 published". Option 2 would count `created_at` instead and stop moves from mattering.

## 2026-11-04 — M17 — Publishing without a review

**Question:** Is an event public at once, or does staff review it first, as a school listing (M11)?

**Options:**
1. **Public at once. Only the organizer changes or cancels it; a cancelled event stays in the calendar marked "Cancelled" (in words and with a crossed-square mark, never colour alone). Staff with a verified second factor remove an event with a reason (audited); a removed event is gone from every list, its page shows "This event is not listed" (noindex, as a school that is not listed) and the API and the `.ics` answer 404.**
2. A review queue before publication (a new case kind in `/admin/review`).
3. Public at once, with a report button for visitors (M10b reports) feeding the review queue.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`createEvent`, `cancelEvent`, `removeEvent` in `src/server/services/events.ts`; marked `DEFAULT M17 (question 2)`). Reports on events are out of scope for M17 (milestone file). The page of a removed event answers 200 with noindex, not 404: a true 404 would need `src/proxy.ts`, which CLAUDE.md §13 limits to the two person paths (ADR 0008, ADR 0014 §"not listed").

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-04 — M17 — Who is told about an event, and how

**Question:** The specification gives Premium Student "Browse and notifications". Who is notified of which events, and through what?

**Options:**
1. **A dashboard notification (no e-mail) to every Premium Student (the effective plan, a test grant included) whose established teacher (a relationship `verified` or `in_arbitration`) is the event's organizer or one of its instructors, when the event is published and when it is cancelled; one per person and event and change. Sent by a background job (pg-boss, `events.notify`) with a singleton key.**
2. The same, by e-mail as well.
3. Every Premium Student in the event's country.
4. Notifications that a student subscribes to (a follow button on a teacher's profile).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`sendEventNotices` in `src/server/jobs/event-notify.ts`, the templates `event.published` and `event.cancelled` in `src/server/templates/notifications.ts`; marked `DEFAULT M17 (question 3)`). A change of date or place sends nothing (only publication and cancellation). A cancellation is sent only to the students who were told of the publication (an event cancelled before its notices went out announces nothing) and never for an event that is already over. Free Students and pending requests get nothing.

**Answer:** (filled in by the owner)

## 2026-11-04 — M17 — The fifth tab

**Question:** The shell has four destinations (Explore, Lineage, Schools, Me). Where does the event calendar go?

**Options:**
1. **A fifth destination, Events, between Schools and Me, in the phone's bottom tabs and the desktop navigation. On a 320 px phone at 150 % text the labels are set an eighth smaller in the narrowest tabs so every label stays one word on one line.**
2. A link under Explore (no new tab).
3. A section of the home page only.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`NAV_ITEMS` in `src/components/shell/nav.ts`, the label queries in `src/components/shell/nav-links.tsx`; marked `DEFAULT M17 (question 4)`). Every visual baseline shows the shell, so they all change; they are accepted only from CI (`scripts/visual-baselines.sh`).

## 2026-11-04 — M17 — Federation's "unlimited publishing" (D25, "unlimited" resources)

**Question:** The specification gives Federation "Unlimited publishing and community push", with limits still to be defined (D25). What applies in the pilot?

**Options:**
1. **No limit for an organization that holds Federation (a grant, M15); an organization without it cannot publish (`ENTITLEMENT_REQUIRED`). "Community push" is not built (no newsletters or social posts, out of scope).**
2. A high fixed limit (for example 20 a month).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`checkOrganization` in `src/server/services/events.ts`, the matrix note in `src/server/entitlements/plans.ts`; marked `DEFAULT D25`).
