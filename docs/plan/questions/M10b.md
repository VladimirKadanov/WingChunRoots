## 2026-11-01 — M10b — The legal text of the invitation e-mail (GDPR Art. 14)

**Question:** the milestone's stop-and-ask condition. The invitation goes to someone who has no account and never gave us their address: the student did. Under GDPR Art. 14 (data not obtained from the data subject) the recipient must be told who the controller is, why the address is processed, on what legal basis, how long it is kept and what their rights are. The M02 drafts (`/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy`, "Draft — pending legal review", DEFAULT D23) were written for people who sign up. Do they cover an invitee, or does the invitation need its own legal text?

**Options:**
1. **The invitation uses only the M02 drafts, linked by their full address, and says in plain words what it can say without new legal text:** a named student gave us the address and listed them as their teacher in a named lineage; nothing about the record is public; the link works for 14 days and once; ignoring the e-mail publishes nothing; the address is deleted 30 days after the invitation ends (`retention.purge`). The address is used for this one e-mail only, never written to a log or an audit row, and the student cannot see it again in the app.
2. A short invitation privacy notice (controller, purpose, legal basis — likely legitimate interest of the student and the platform —, retention, the right to object and to erasure, a contact), reviewed by a lawyer, linked from the e-mail and shown on `/invite/<token>`.
3. No e-mail from WingChunRoots at all: the student gets a link to pass on themselves, so the platform never processes the teacher's address.

**Proposed default:** option 1 (`src/server/templates/email/invitation.tsx`, `docs/evidence-and-review.md` "Покани, поемане на профил…", `docs/adr/0013-claims-reports-arbitration.md` §2). Option 2 needs the text from a lawyer before real participants (D23 is due before M13); option 3 changes the service (`relationships.invite` would return a link instead of queuing an e-mail).

## 2026-11-01 — M10b — A teacher who already has an account and a profile

**Question:** an invitation can only be accepted by an account that has no profile of its own (one profile per account, `person.claimed_by_user_id` is unique). If the invited teacher already signed up and has a profile, the invitation page says: "Your account already has its own profile, so it cannot claim another one. Ask the student to send their request to your profile instead." The student's unclaimed record then stays (involved_only, never public) until the student withdraws the request. Should the reviewers be able to merge the unclaimed record into the teacher's existing profile instead?

**Options:**
1. **No merge in Phase 1: refuse with that message; the student withdraws the request to the unclaimed record and sends a new one to the existing profile (M09).** Nothing public changes and no record is joined by hand.
2. A merge tool for archivists: the request moves to the existing profile, the unclaimed record is deleted, audited as `person.merge`. Needs rules for names, evidence and other requests that point at the unclaimed record.

**Proposed default:** option 1 (`src/server/services/invitations.ts` `refusalFor`; `docs/guides/user-guide.bg.md`).

## 2026-11-01 — M10b — Who decides profile reports, and what the reporter is told

**Question:** plan §6.2 adds "Report profile" and §6.7 gives moderators the disputes; neither says who decides a profile report or whether the reporter hears the outcome.

**Options:**
1. **A moderator or an administrator upholds or dismisses the report in `/admin/review`; nothing is hidden automatically; the reporter sees the state of their reports through `reports.mine` (no e-mail).** Correcting the profile is an archival edit (an archivist) or an administrator's action.
2. The same, and the reporter also gets a notification when the report is decided.
3. Archivists decide reports about the record's facts (misleading, copyright), moderators the others (spam, inappropriate).

**Proposed default:** option 1 (`src/server/services/review-core.ts` `DECIDERS.profile_report`, `src/server/services/reports.ts`; a default with no D number, `docs/evidence-and-review.md`).
