M18 did not stop on these questions: each keeps its proposed default, marked in the code and the docs (`DEFAULT M18 (question N)` and docs/adr/0021-notices.md), until the owner answers it.

## 2026-11-05 — M18 — Which notices are e-mailed by default, and which can be switched off

**Question:** Every notice appears in the dashboard. Which categories are also e-mailed when the account has made no choice, and which can the account switch off?

**Options:**
1. **Dashboard always on, never a choice. E-mail per category in /dashboard/settings: on by default for security, account, plan, teaching ("Teachers and students"), review, schools and organizations; off by default for events (as in M17). Security and account (legal notices: deletion, removal by the moderators) are always e-mailed and cannot be switched off. The limit notice is never e-mailed (question 2).**
2. The same, with schools and organizations off by default (the milestone file names only security, account, plan, teaching and review as "on").
3. Every category off by default except security and account (opt-in for everything else).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`CATEGORIES` and `NOTIFICATION_CATALOG` in `src/server/templates/catalog.ts`; `notification_preference` with `CHECK notification_preference_locked_categories`). Schools and organizations are on because their notices are the answer to the account's own request (a listing or an application reviewed), like teaching and review. A dashboard-only kind of M02–M17 stays dashboard-only, except the four that M18 adds an e-mail to (question 4).

## 2026-11-05 — M18 — The notice at a limit of the plan

**Question:** When an action meets a limit of the plan (approved students D06, photos D07, events a month M17), how often is the account told, and how?

**Options:**
1. **One dashboard notice per feature and calendar month (UTC), key `limit.reached:<feature>:<YYYY-MM>`, only for a used-up allowance (`requireEntitlement` refusal `quota`), never for a feature the plan does not include at all (`plan`). With the upgrade prompt of M08 (only benefits with `availability: 'now'`). Never e-mailed. The refusal rolls the action back, so the notice is written after it, in its own transaction; a notice that cannot be written never changes the refusal.**
2. One notice per feature until the limit is lifted (a plan change), however many months pass.
3. A notice at every refusal.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/services/limit-notices.ts`; `approveRelationship`, `uploadPhoto`, `addGalleryPhoto`, `createEvent`, `updateEvent`). Professional Sifu at its monthly events limit gets the notice without an upgrade (there is no higher plan for an account).

## 2026-11-05 — M18 — How long notices are kept

**Question:** How long does the dashboard keep a notice?

**Options:**
1. **A read notice is deleted 180 days after it was created, an unread one after 365, by the existing daily `retention.purge` job (in batches, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`). The record of sent e-mails keeps the rules of M01b.**
2. Keep every notice while the account exists.
3. 90 days for every notice.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`purgeOldNotifications` in `src/server/services/retention.ts`, `READ_NOTIFICATION_RETENTION_DAYS`, `UNREAD_NOTIFICATION_RETENTION_DAYS`).

## 2026-11-05 — M18 — The new e-mails of existing notices

**Question:** The milestone adds e-mails for "relationship ended, dispute resolved, school or organization review accepted, invitation accepted". For which decisions and stages?

**Options:**
1. **`relationship.ended`, `invitation.claimed` (every stage: accepted, approved, refused), `school.reviewed` and `organization.reviewed` (every decision, not only "accepted": a refusal with its reason matters as much) go out as the generic notice e-mail (`src/server/templates/email/notice.tsx`, the notice's own text). A resolved dispute was already e-mailed since M10b (`relationship-arbitration`, DEFAULT D19) and stays so.**
2. Only the accepting decisions (approved listing, approved organization, accepted invitation).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`NOTIFICATION_CATALOG` in `src/server/templates/catalog.ts`).

## 2026-11-05 — M18 — Moderation notices: what is covered

**Question:** "Moderation removal with the reason" — which removals exist to notify?

**Options:**
1. **The removals staff can do today: an event (M17, to the organizer or the creator), a profile photo or a gallery photo (M14, M16, to the account that owns the person, never the staff member's own), and a school photo (M16, to the account that listed the school). One `content.removed` notice per item (key `content.removed:<kind>:<id>`), with the staff's reason and never the staff member's name, in the category `account` (a statement of reasons: always e-mailed). Staff cannot remove a school listing or an organization outright (they are reviewed: `school.reviewed`, `organization.reviewed`), so there is nothing more to notify.**
2. Also a removal path for schools and organizations in M18.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`removeEvent` in `src/server/services/events.ts`, `tellPhotoOwner` in `src/server/services/photos.ts`, `src/server/services/school-photos.ts`).

## 2026-11-05 — M18 — The e-mail budget and the end of a plan

**Question:** Two smaller defaults: how many notice e-mails one account may get, and which plan ends are announced.

**Options:**
1. **At most 20 e-mails of one template to one account in an hour (`NOTICE_EMAIL_HOURLY_CAP`, the rule of M02's account e-mails, counted under an advisory lock); over it the notice stays in the dashboard and only its e-mail drops. The daily `plans.expiry` job (06:41 UTC) announces the end of an account's own grants (not an organization's): once when it ends within 7 days, once when it has ended (an end within the last 7 days, so a missed day catches up). A revoked grant is not announced (it had `plan.revoked`), nor one carried on by another grant of the same plan. The text gives the date and what stays, never a countdown.**
2. Other numbers, or the organization's grants too (to its administrators).

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied (`src/server/services/notify.ts`, `src/server/jobs/plan-expiry.ts`).
