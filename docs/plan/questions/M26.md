# M26 — questions for the owner

M26 did not stop on these questions. Each one keeps its proposed default until the owner answers it. The defaults are recorded in docs/adr/0034-archive-editor.md.

## 2026-11-17 — M26 — 1. Who keeps the archive

**Question:** The plan (§6.6) lets only an archivist edit archival records. An account holds one role, and the two people of stage 2 are administrators. Who may create and edit lineages, historical records and links?

**Options:**
1. **An archivist or an administrator with a verified second factor, for the archive editor and for the archival edit of M10a (`ARCHIVE_EDITOR_ROLES` in `src/server/services/archive-editor.ts`).**
2. Archivists only, as in the plan: an administrator hands the other the archivist role for data entry, and that account loses its admin rights.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied. Asked on a decision card in the thread on 2026-11-17.

**Answer:** option 1, "Archivists and admins" (the owner, decision card, 2026-11-17).

## 2026-11-17 — M26 — 2. One person or two for a link

**Question:** A link entered in the archive editor is verified at once, on the staff member's source. Should a second member of staff confirm it first ("four eyes")?

**Options:**
1. **One staff member verifies it, as one reviewer verifies a historical claim (M10a). The source is required, the evidence optional, and every link is audited and can be ended with a reason.**
2. The link waits as `under_review` until another archivist or administrator confirms it.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied.

**Answer:** option 1, "One staff member" (the owner, decision card, 2026-11-27).

## 2026-11-17 — M26 — 3. Whose links are entered here

**Question:** May staff enter a link for a member who has an account (for example, the historical teacher of a living member)?

**Options:**
1. **No: both ends must be records kept by the platform or unclaimed. A member's links are confirmed by their teacher (M09) or by a claim with evidence (M10a), so nobody publishes a member without their consent (DEFAULT D03).**
2. Yes, when the member is the student, with a notice to the member.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied.

**Answer:** option 1, "No, archive records only" (the owner, decision card, 2026-11-27).

## 2026-11-17 — M26 — 4. The earliest year of a training period

**Question:** Requests refuse a training period before 1900 (`EARLIEST_TRAINING_YEAR`). Historical links are older. Which floor does the archive editor use?

**Options:**
1. **1600 (`ARCHIVE_EARLIEST_YEAR`), with years only (no month or day); both years empty means the dates are not known.**
2. No floor.

**Proposed default:** option 1, applied.

**Answer:** option 1, "1600, years only" (the owner, decision card, 2026-11-27).
