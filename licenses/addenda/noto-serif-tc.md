The brand mark of WingChunRoots (a red seal with the characters 詠春根, "Wing Chun Roots", and a
square seal with 根, "root") contains the outlines of three glyphs, U+8A60 詠, U+6625 春 and
U+6839 根, traced from **Noto Serif TC Bold** as published in `@fontsource/noto-serif-tc` 5.2.9
(files `noto-serif-tc-106-700-normal.woff2`, `noto-serif-tc-116-700-normal.woff2` and
`noto-serif-tc-115-700-normal.woff2`). The outlines are embedded as SVG paths in
`src/components/brand/brand-mark.tsx` and `src/app/icon.svg`, scaled and positioned; the font
itself is not shipped and is not a dependency.
