The brand mark of WingChunRoots (a red seal with the character 根, "root") contains the
outline of one glyph, U+6839 根, traced from **Noto Serif TC Bold** as published in
`@fontsource/noto-serif-tc` 5.2.9 (file `noto-serif-tc-115-700-normal.woff2`). The outline is
embedded as an SVG path in `src/components/brand/brand-mark.tsx`, scaled and positioned; the
font itself is not shipped and is not a dependency.
