> **Draft — pending legal review.** This is a working draft for the WingChunRoots pilot. A lawyer has not reviewed it yet, and it will change before the pilot opens to the public. When it changes, you will be asked to accept the new version.

## 1. What WingChunRoots is

WingChunRoots records Wing Chun teaching lineages: who taught whom, confirmed by the people involved or by evidence. It also has a school finder and a training passport. This is a pilot: a small, local trial with a limited number of members.

## 2. Adults only

The pilot is open only to people who are **18 or older**. When you create an account, you confirm that you are 18 or older. We do not ask for your birth date. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data.

## 3. Your account

- One account per person. The e-mail address must be yours, and you confirm it before you can sign in.
- Keep your password to yourself. We recommend turning on two-factor authentication.
- Moderators, archivists and administrators must use two-factor authentication.
- Your account is not your profile. The profile is the record of you as a practitioner; it starts private, and it becomes public only if you choose so.

## 4. Authenticity declaration

When you create an account, you declare that:

- you will record only what you believe to be true;
- you will not claim a teacher, a student, a rank or a date that is not yours, and you will not invent people or events;
- you will not record a claim on behalf of another living person without their agreement;
- you will provide evidence privately when a reviewer asks for it, and you will not upload documents you have no right to share.

A claim between living people stays private to the people involved until it is confirmed. A disputed claim goes to review, and to arbitration when needed. We may correct or remove records that break this declaration.

## 5. What is not allowed

- Pretending to be someone else, or creating accounts for other people.
- Harassing, threatening or shaming anyone, in a claim, a note or a report.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address, a phone number or a document.
- Collecting data from the site in bulk, or trying to get around its security or its limits.

## 6. Ending your account

You can ask us to close your account at any time. Records that other people have confirmed may stay, without your account details, when they describe a teaching relationship that others rely on; you can ask us to hide your name from them.

## 7. Changes

We may update these terms. A new version has a new date. You will be asked to accept it before you continue to use your account. We keep a record of which version you accepted and when.

## 8. Contact

During the pilot, write to the pilot team. The contact address will be published here before the pilot opens to the public.
