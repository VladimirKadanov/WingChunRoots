## 1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is the WingChunRoots pilot team: **[controller name, to be added before anyone outside the pilot team joins]**. Write to **[contact e-mail address, to be added]** about anything in this notice. We answer within one month.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account:** your e-mail address; your password, stored only as a salted hash; your role; whether you chose Student or Sifu; your time zone; when you were last active; whether two-factor authentication is on, with its secret and backup codes stored encrypted.
- **Sessions:** a session token in a cookie, its expiry date, your browser's user-agent text and the `ip_hmac` value described below.
- **Profile:** your names (in Latin letters and, if you add them, in their original script, Jyutping and pinyin), your country, your city, the year you started training, a biography, links and videos you add, your profile photos and gallery, and whether your profile is public.
- **Teaching links and the archive:** the teachers and students you name or confirm, the notes on your requests, claims of a record as your own, invitations you send (the invited person's name and e-mail address), evidence files you upload for a review, the reviewers' notes and decisions, and the history of changes to a record.
- **Passport:** ranks, examinations, skills, certificates (the scanned file) and events you attended, written by you, your teacher or an organization.
- **Schools, events and organizations:** the schools you list (with their contact details, schedule and photos), the events you organize (with their poster), the organizations you administer, and where you are listed as a member, an instructor or an examiner.
- **Trial lessons:** when you ask a school for a trial lesson, your name and the contact detail you chose (your e-mail address or a phone number), the times and message you wrote, and the school's reply. A school's page views are counted per day, without anything about the visitor.
- **Plans and payments:** your plan, its period and, for a subscription, its state. During the pilot payments run in test mode only: no card details reach us or anyone else.
- **Notices and e-mail:** the notices on your dashboard, your notification settings, and a log of the e-mails we sent you.
- **Confirmations:** which confirmation you gave (age, terms, authenticity declaration, privacy notice, a public profile), for which version of the document, and when.

We do **not** ask for your birth date. The pilot is for adults only, and you confirm your age with a tick.

**People without an account.** A record of a teacher or student who has no account (for example someone a student names, or a historical teacher) holds their names, their place in a lineage and what the archive's reviewers checked. Such a person can ask us to correct or remove it (section 8).

## 3. How we treat your IP address (ip_hmac)

We do not store your IP address. Where an address helps protect accounts (your sessions and the audit log of changes), we store `ip_hmac` instead: a keyed fingerprint of your IP address (HMAC-SHA-256 with a secret key that only the server knows). It shows that two events came from the same address, but the address cannot be read back from it. The key is replaced every year, and values older than 13 months are deleted.

To limit repeated attempts, the server counts failed sign-ins per e-mail address (and, for a browser that has signed in to that address before, per browser) and remembers the sign-in codes it has just accepted. It stores only keyed fingerprints of the e-mail address, the browser and the code, never the address or the code itself. They expire after 15 minutes (2 minutes for the codes) and are deleted at the next daily clean-up, within a day. Requests for confirmation and reset e-mails are counted per IP address in the server's memory for a minute, without writing the address anywhere, and the e-mails each account gets are counted per hour in the log of sent e-mail.

## 4. Why we use it, and on what basis

- **To run your account and the features you use** (profile, teaching links, passport, schools, events, organizations, trial lessons, plans): because you asked for them (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **To keep accounts and the archive safe** (sign-in limits, the audit log, reviews of claims and reports): our legitimate interest in a trustworthy service (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **To keep a record of teaching lineages**, including people without an account: our legitimate interest in documenting the history of the art (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to it (section 8).
- **To show your profile publicly:** only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Privacy settings.
- **To keep the records the law asks for** (for example of payments, once real payments start): a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

## 5. Who receives it

- **Other members** see what you make public, and the people you are linked with see the link and its state. A school you ask for a trial lesson sees the name and contact detail you chose. An organization you are a member of, and its administrators, see your membership.
- **OpenFreeMap** draws the maps of the school finder. When you look at a map, your browser loads the map images directly from OpenFreeMap, which therefore sees your IP address and the part of the map you view. No account data is sent to it.
- **YouTube and Vimeo** play a video on a profile only after you press its play button; then your browser loads the player from them, and they see your IP address.
- **Hosting, e-mail, payment and monitoring providers** will be listed here when they are chosen. During the pilot the site runs on a server the pilot team rents in the European Union, and e-mail goes to a test mailbox on that server; no message leaves it.

We do not sell data, and we do not use advertising or tracking services.

## 6. Who can see your profile

Your profile starts private: only the people involved in a teaching link with you and the reviewers can see it. It becomes public only if you tick "Show my profile publicly", and a confirmed teaching link shows your name publicly only if you did. A claim between living people stays private to the people involved until it is confirmed. Nobody can list you as a member of an organization, an instructor at an event or the examiner of a grading without your agreement: you get a request and can accept or decline it. Exact school addresses are never shown publicly.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Your account and profile: until you ask us to delete the account. An account whose e-mail address was never confirmed is deleted, with its confirmations, 7 days after sign-up.
- Sessions: until they expire or you sign out.
- E-mail addresses in the log of sent e-mail: 90 days. The address on an invitation: 30 days after it ended.
- Notices: 180 days after you read them, 365 days if you never do.
- Trial-lesson requests: 365 days after they closed; an open request nobody answered is closed after 90 days.
- A record made for someone else who never confirmed it: 60 days after the claim or invitation ended. Evidence and notes of a refused or withdrawn claim: 90 days after it closed.
- The text in the history of a record's changes: 13 months. The audit log of changes is kept without names or e-mail addresses.
- Daily school page counts: 400 days.

## 8. Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your data, receive a copy, restrict a use of it or object to it, and withdraw a consent at any time.

- **A copy:** press **Download my data** in Privacy settings for one file with everything we keep about your account and profile.
- **Correct:** edit your profile, or ask us.
- **Delete:** press **Request account deletion** in Privacy settings; an administrator carries it out.
- **Object, or a record about you without an account:** write to us (section 1), or use **Report** on the page.

You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД, www.cpdp.bg) or to the data protection authority of the country you live in.

## 9. Changes to this notice

When this notice changes, the new version gets a new date, and you are asked to read it the next time you open your account.

## 10. Cookies and browser storage

- `wcr.session_token`: keeps you signed in. Necessary.
- `wcr.two_factor`: holds a sign-in for a few minutes while you enter your second factor. Necessary.
- `wcr.form`: when your browser sends a form without JavaScript, holds the form's answer (its messages and the values you typed, never a password or a code) for 2 minutes, so the page can show it. Necessary.
- `wcr.sign-in-email`: after you confirm your e-mail address, fills it into the sign-in form for 10 minutes. Sent only to the sign-in page. Necessary.
- `wcr.codes`: holds your new backup codes, encrypted, for 30 minutes while you save them. Sent only to the security page. Necessary.
- `wcr.known_device`: remembers that this browser has signed in to your account before (or set its new password), so that wrong passwords someone else types for your e-mail address never lock you out here. It holds a keyed fingerprint of the address and of your current password, and a random number, not the address itself, for 180 days; a new password makes it count for nothing, except in the browser that set it. Necessary.
- `wcr.totp-setup`: holds the key of a two-factor setup you started, encrypted, for 30 minutes, so the security page can show it again after a reload or a trip to your authenticator app. Sent only to the security page. Necessary.
- `wcr-theme`: remembers the light or dark theme if you choose one.
- `wcr.admin_lang`: for administrators only, remembers whether the control panel is shown in Bulgarian or English.
- `wcr.recent-searches` (browser storage, not a cookie): your last searches, kept only in this browser and cleared when you sign out.

There are no advertising or analytics cookies.
